RESULT SUMMARY

Models tested:
- Qwen (via Ollama)
- LLaVA (via Ollama)

Testing method:
- Inference on a small set of plant leaf images

Observations:
- Models were able to generate generic descriptions
- Plant and disease prediction was inconsistent
- Most images were NOT predicted correctly
- Fine-grained disease identification was poor

Conclusion:
Ollama-based VLMs are not reliable for plant leaf disease detection
without domain-specific fine-tuning.
